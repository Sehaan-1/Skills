# Visual Style Archetypes

A style archetype provides a cohesive baseline for layout dials, palette family, typographic stance, and aesthetic constraints. Select one primary archetype per product in `DESIGN.md`.

## The Nine Archetypes

| Archetype | Best For | Boldness | Motion | Density | Palette Family | Typography Stance | Banned Elements |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Keep** | B2B software, management tools, dashboards | 3 | 2 | 6 | Clean neutral ramp, single accent | Clean grotesk sans, weight-driven hierarchy | Extraneous decoration, full-bleed hero, gradients |
| **Scriptorium** | Documentation, knowledge bases, long-form content | 2 | 2 | 4 | Paper neutrals, high-contrast ink | Body serif permitted, mono for code/IDs | Card-inside-card nests, accent sprawl |
| **Cathedral** | Editorial publications, luxury brands, storytelling | 6 | 3 | 2 | High contrast monochrome with paper textures | Expressive display serif, tall typography scale | Card grids, drop shadows, decorative icons |
| **Barbican** | Developer tools, builder products, manifestos | 5 | 4 | 7 | Near-black on off-white, single alert hue | Heavy grotesk caps against mono | Rounded pill corners, blur, soft shadows |
| **Armoury** | Terminals, system telemetry, consoles | 4 | 5 | 9 | Dark charcoal surfaces, single emissive accent | Monospace for IDs and metrics, grotesk for UI | Serif fonts, light mode default, pastels |
| **Watchtower** | Ops monitoring, incident response, live data | 3 | 4 | 8 | High-clarity dark or light, semantic alerts only | Tabular numerals everywhere | Decorative animations, illustrations |
| **Solar** | Warm consumer apps, health, hospitality | 5 | 5 | 4 | Warm organic neutrals, deliberate accent | Humanist sans, warm geometric display | Purple glow, beige-and-brass reflex |
| **Bazaar** | Consumer social, events, entertainment | 9 | 8 | 5 | Saturated primary hues against neutral ground | Chunky display typeface, high weight contrast | Dull corporate blue, monochrome monotony |
| **Masquerade** | Creative agencies, design portfolios, product launches | 9 | 9 | 2 | Stark bone or deep black, single loud accent | Oversized display typography carrying the grid | Card-based layouts, stock photography |

---

## Guidelines

1. **One archetype per product:** Using two disparate styles creates visual incoherence. A split is only justified across a clear boundary (e.g., a marketing landing page in *Cathedral* and the authenticated app in *Keep*).
2. **Design for sustained use:** Pick the style that remains comfortable after three hours of daily use, not merely what looks flashy in a single screenshot.
